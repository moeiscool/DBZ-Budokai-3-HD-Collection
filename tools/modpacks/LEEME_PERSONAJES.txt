DBZ BUDOKAI 3 HD COLLECTION - NEW CHARACTERS PACK (v1.4.0)
==========================================================

Seven NEW characters in their own cells of the select wheel (they replace nobody):
Janemba, Android 19, Zarbon (with Monster Form), Dodoria, Guldo, Jeice and Burter.
Zarbon and Dodoria also bring the moves, combos and yells of their Budokai 1 version.

YOU NEED: version 1.4.0 (or later) of DBZ Budokai 3 HD Collection, with the game data
in a FOLDER (mods are not applied in ISO mode).

INSTALL (3 steps)
1. Close the game.
2. Copy this ZIP's "mods" folder next to dbz3.exe (if asked, accept merging the
   folders: nothing of yours is deleted).
3. Open dbz3.exe, check the "New characters" tab (you will see the 7 ticked) and press
   PLAY. They are now on the select wheel.

REMOVING A CHARACTER
- In the launcher, "New characters" tab > "Installed": untick its box and press PLAY.
  To delete it completely, remove its "cut_..." folder from mods.
- Your saves are never touched.

NOTES
- These are experimental characters: some Budokai 1 grab techniques play as a normal
  hit, and some effects may not be perfect.
- The "_roster" folder is the already-built result of these 7. If you also have the
  Modding kit installed, the launcher rebuilds it by itself when you change something.
- Experimental PS5 build (US executable only): the pack should work by copying "mods"
  to /data/dbz3/mods on the console, but this is untested. See docs/PS5.md in the
  source repository.
